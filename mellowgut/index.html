MellowGut

A calm food and symptom diary for IBS

Log meals, symptoms, bowel movements, stress and sleep, and MellowGut looks for the foods that tend to come before your symptoms — so you have something concrete to test, or to take to your GP.

  • Your diary stays on your phone. There is no account and nothing to sign in to.
  • Patterns are found on the phone, from your own entries.
  • A report you can share with your doctor, and exports you control.

MellowGut isn't a medical device and can't diagnose conditions. Talk to your GP about any new or worrying symptoms.
