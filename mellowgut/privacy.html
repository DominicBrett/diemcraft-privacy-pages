MellowGut

Privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026

This policy explains what information the MellowGut app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. MellowGut is made by Dominic Brett ("we", "us"). If you have any question about it, email mellowgutibsapp@gmail.com.

In short
  • Your diary stays on your phone. We never receive, share or sell anything you log.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. They are never personalised, and nothing you log is used for them.
  • Premium is bought through Google Play or the App Store, with RevenueCat handling the subscription. They see your purchases, never your diary.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter in MellowGut is stored only on your device: meals and drinks, symptoms, bowel movements, stress, sleep, periods, medication, notes, recipes, the name you give the app, and your settings. The patterns MellowGut finds are worked out on the phone from those entries. We have no server that holds a copy, so we cannot see, recover or hand over your diary.

Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. No notification is sent from, or through, a server of ours.

What leaves your phone

Ads (free version)

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show an ad, Google's software in the app sends Google information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and operating system, the app's name, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure them and prevent fraud.

Every ad request asks for non-personalised ads only, so Google chooses ads from the app and your general location rather than from a profile of you. MellowGut does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Nothing from your diary is ever included in an ad request.

Where the law requires it, including in the UK and the European Economic Area, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad is requested. You can change it at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Premium removes ads entirely.

Google's handling of this information is covered by Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Premium)

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or Apple's App Store. We never see your card or payment details.

We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and to know whether Premium is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier created for the app, your purchase and subscription history, and basic device and store information such as your operating system, country and currency. It is not given your name, your email address or anything from your diary. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Files you choose to share

When you export a backup or spreadsheet, or share the report for your doctor, MellowGut creates the file on your phone and hands it to the app you pick. Where it goes from there is up to you, and is covered by that app's own policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your health information. Your diary is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • We don't sell or rent any information.
  • We don't use analytics, crash reporting or other tracking tools.
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

Legal basis

Under UK and EU data protection law, the information Google AdMob receives is processed with your consent where the law requires consent, and otherwise in our legitimate interest in funding a free app with non-personalised ads. Purchase information is processed to provide the Premium you bought.

How long information is kept, and deleting it

Your diary stays until you delete it. Settings → Clear all diary entries deletes your entries; Settings → Reset app and start over deletes everything MellowGut stores; and uninstalling the app removes it all from the phone. There is no copy anywhere else, so none of this can be undone.

Google and RevenueCat keep the information they receive for as long as their own policies set out. If you would like us to ask RevenueCat to delete your purchase record, email us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no diary or account data, most requests are best handled in the app itself or with Google or RevenueCat, but we're happy to help: email mellowgutibsapp@gmail.com. In the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

MellowGut is made for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Not medical advice

MellowGut spots patterns in your own entries. It isn't a medical device and can't diagnose conditions. Talk to your GP about any new or worrying symptoms.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects what leaves your phone, we will also say so in the app.
